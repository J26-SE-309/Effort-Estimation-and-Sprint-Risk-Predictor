# Risk labels (R1–R6)

Generated by `erp-build-labels`. For every snapshot story, which of the six warning signs from proposal Appendix B happened in its first sprint. A story is *at risk* (M2's answer) when any rule fires; the risk level is low / medium / high for 0 / 1 / 2+ rules. These are automatic labels: they still need the 200-story human check (ML guide 6.3) before anything is trained on them.

## 1. The rules as implemented

| Rule | How it is computed |
|---|---|
| R1 Spillover: not done by the end of the sprint | The story was not resolved when the sprint closed (+ clock tolerance), replaying the resolution history, so a story resolved and then reopened before the close counts; or it was moved on to the next sprint unresolved at the close. |
| R2 Delayed closure: done only after the sprint ended, still counted in it | R1, and the story was resolved later while still recorded in that sprint (not carried over). |
| R3 Re-estimation: story points changed during the sprint | A `Story Points` change that alters the value, after the commitment estimate and before the sprint closed (or before the story moved to its next sprint). |
| R4 Blocked: an open blocker for more than 30% of the sprint | Time with an 'is blocked by' / 'depends on' link to an unresolved issue, while the story was in the sprint and unresolved, divided by the sprint length. Link dates come from the `Change_Log`. |
| R5 Carry-over: carried into the next sprint unfinished | The story was committed to the following sprint (starting before the end of one more sprint length) while it was unresolved. |
| R6 Reopening: reopened after being done, in the sprint or the next one | Status set to Reopened, or the resolution cleared, after it was first resolved and before the end of the following sprint (close + one sprint length). |

## 2. How often each rule fires

Stories: 22,115. **At risk: 10,669 (48.2%)**.

| R3 Re-estimation: story points changed during the sprint | 1,308 | 5.9% |

Risk level: low 11,446 (51.8%), medium 2,284 (10.3%), high 8,385 (37.9%).

R1 without the clock tolerance (resolved by the recorded close exactly): 43.9%, against 42.5% with it.
