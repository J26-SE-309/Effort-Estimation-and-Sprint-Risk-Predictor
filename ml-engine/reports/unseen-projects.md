# Predictions for projects the models never saw

Generated by `erp-unseen-projects` (`erp/arena/unseen.py`). Leave one project out: for each of the 32 TAWOS projects with test stories, `fasttext-lightgbm` (the router's configuration, its tuned settings) was trained without the project, whose key it then did not know (as for a platform project), and predicted all its stories; the same configuration trained with the project predicted its test stories. Evidence for the confidence score's unseen-project penalty (x0.7) and cold-start penalty (x0.7, fewer than 3 closed sprints).

| Group | Stories | Effort MAE (points) | Risk Brier | Risk ROC-AUC | Risk ECE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| seen, test stories | 3,333 | 1.758 | 0.2262 | 0.679 | 0.032 |
| unseen, test stories | 3,333 | 1.990 | 0.2427 | 0.645 | 0.093 |
| unseen, cold (< 3 sprints) | 533 | 3.434 | 0.2658 | 0.569 | 0.158 |
| unseen, warm (>= 3 sprints) | 20,294 | 2.324 | 0.2360 | 0.662 | 0.078 |

The cost of being unseen, on the same test stories (paired bootstrap; for MAE and Brier, positive means worse when unseen):

| Risk ROC-AUC | -0.0340 | -0.0486 to -0.0205 | 0.001 |
